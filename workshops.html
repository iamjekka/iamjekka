Transpose

A space for shared learning and artistic exchange.

Transpose is a Berlin-based music collective co-founded by two female producers, JEKKA and MIN t, with extensive experience in music production, performance and the creative industries.

We created Transpose to build a space where music is not only produced but shared, discussed and experienced collectively. Our goal is to encourage collaboration, artistic exchange and lifelong learning while making the music community more open, accessible and connected.

Group workshops and collaborative learning formats are offered through Transpose.
