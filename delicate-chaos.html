Delicate Chaos

Delicate Chaos is just as it sounds: a chaotic ride through emotional turmoil of a life falling apart and beliefs collapsing. In times of war music is perhaps the only way I can express my deepest sorrow, the crushing pain of lost lives and severed ties, exile and isolation. The story follows the 5 stages of grief mourning the loss of identity, place and ideals and the search for my voice. It is a quest to understand how to live in the time of Monsters and stay human.

Sonically the record spans many genres and this is intentional: I allowed myself to go all out and explore different productions following just the emotional and narrative theme. Each song is like a scene from a film constructed in real time, slightly messy, overdramatic and even naive. Scoring dystopian nightmares and nostalgic dreams, stuck somewhere in limbo.

When the credits roll, there’s a call to action and much work to do. A refusal to let hope die and the monsters win.
