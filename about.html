About

JEKKA

Jekka is a Berlin-based artist, producer and composer working across dark pop, cinematic electronic music and interdisciplinary sound. Active since 2012, her work moves between songwriting, electronic production, performance and conceptual projects, often exploring identity, belonging, memory, transformation and the experience of living between cultures.

Her practice combines intuitive songwriting with research-led approaches to sound and world-building. Projects range from #ГОД (YEAR), a four-part reimagining of Slavic folk traditions through electronic music, to her 2026 album Delicate Chaos, which follows the five stages of grief through shifting genres, dystopian imagery and nostalgic dreamscapes.

Alongside her own releases, Jekka collaborates with filmmakers, visual artists and designers, and creates music and sound for performance, fashion and moving image.
